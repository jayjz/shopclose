# ShopClose — Product Definition

## One-sentence positioning
ShopClose helps HVAC contractors identify and resolve possible billing omissions before completed service jobs are finalized.

## Target customer
Initial hypothesis:
- Independent residential or light-commercial HVAC service contractor
- Approximately 5–25 field technicians
- Existing FSM or structured billing workflow
- Office staff responsible for reconciling service notes and invoices
- Enough service volume for exception handling to become repetitive

Initial discovery geography: Lowell, Massachusetts, and surrounding northern Massachusetts / southern New Hampshire markets.

These are targeting assumptions, not evidence of demand.

## Buyer
Owner, general manager, service manager, or office manager with responsibility for billing accuracy and operational profitability.

## Job to be done
"When my technicians complete jobs, help me ensure documented billable work is reconciled against the invoice before we lose time chasing notes or leave legitimate charges unresolved."

## Core problem
Technician notes, service activity, and billable line items may disagree.

Examples:
- Refrigerant addition appears in notes but not as an explicit invoice line.
- A replaced component is not clearly accounted for.
- An equipment identifier is missing from the closeout.
- Office staff must contact technicians to clarify completed work.

None of these independently proves a financial loss.

## Initial solution
1. Accept a job record and invoice line items.
2. Identify evidence-supported candidate discrepancies.
3. Explain what was observed and what remains uncertain.
4. Save an auditable review.
5. Route the candidate to an authorized employee.
6. Track resolution without changing the invoice automatically.

## Differentiation hypothesis
Contractor-specific exception detection layered over software the business already owns.

Not another scheduling, dispatching, payment, or general FSM platform.

## Primary success metrics
- Human-confirmed actionable findings
- Precision of candidate findings against independently reviewed examples
- Reviewer time required per finding
- Administrative time saved per closed job
- Verified corrected invoice value where applicable
- Customer willingness to pay and paid retention

## Commercial model to test
- Paid workflow audit
- Fixed-scope setup and configuration
- Monthly monitoring or support

Illustrative discovery prices:
- Audit: $450
- Setup: $1,500–$3,000
- Managed service: $350–$600/month

These are hypotheses, not validated market prices.

## Economic test
Customer benefit should exceed software fees, review labor, implementation costs, and disruption.

A candidate omission is not recovered revenue. Count recovered revenue only after the contractor verifies the correct charge and its actual financial result.

## Initial non-goals
- Full FSM replacement
- Full estimating and takeoffs
- Autonomous invoice edits
- Manufacturer warranty registration
- Distributor pricebook synchronization
- Automatic customer messaging

Future features must be justified by direct customer evidence.

## Validation sequence
Interview → historical job audit → measured findings → supervised pilot → paid engagement → selective automation.

Do not build a broad SaaS platform before proving the first repeatable customer outcome.
