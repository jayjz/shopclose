# ShopClose — HVAC Domain Rules

## Fundamental distinction
A technician mentioning a material or component does not establish that it was installed, billable, omitted, or financially recoverable.

## Service activities
Classify documented activity conservatively:

- **Installed/replaced:** Explicit action such as "replaced failed contactor."
- **Added/consumed:** Explicit material use such as "added 2 lbs R-410A."
- **Inspected/tested:** Measurements or evaluation without installation.
- **Recommended:** Proposed work that may not have occurred.
- **Negated:** Explicitly not performed.
- **Ambiguous:** Insufficient evidence to determine what happened.

Negations and recommendations must not be treated as completed billable work.

## Billing exceptions
Potential reasons a separately described material is not separately billed:
- Flat-rate repair package
- Diagnostic fee including specified materials
- Warranty-covered repair
- Membership benefit or approved discount
- Callback or no-charge visit
- Previously invoiced work
- Part supplied by the customer
- Internal goodwill adjustment
- Shop-specific bundled line item

Do not assert a missing charge before checking known applicable exceptions.

## Example cases

| Technician notes | Invoice | Expected interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Replaced capacitor | Capacitor replacement | No obvious discrepancy |
| Added 2 lbs R-410A | Diagnostic only | Candidate for review |
| Checked capacitor, tested good | Diagnostic only | No replacement claim |
| Did not replace contactor | Diagnostic only | No replacement claim |
| Recommend replacing contactor | Diagnostic only | Proposed work, not completed |
| Replaced capacitor under warranty | Diagnostic only | Review warranty coverage, not presumed lost revenue |
| Replaced capacitor | Flat-rate cooling repair | Resolve against the contractor's pricebook |

## Equipment identification
Model numbers, serial numbers, and AHRI references serve different purposes and must not be conflated.

- Manufacturer model identifies an equipment model.
- Serial identifies a specific equipment unit.
- AHRI reference identifies a certified equipment combination or rating record where applicable.

OCR or vision-derived identifiers are provisional until verified against the actual image or another authoritative record.

## Warranty and rebate boundaries
Warranty eligibility depends on the applicable manufacturer, product, registration requirements, date, ownership, installation, and terms.

Rebate eligibility may depend on utility program, equipment certification, contractor participation, documentation, timing, and preapproval.

Never infer eligibility from an invoice or equipment photo alone.

## Unit economics
Separate:
- Suspected omitted billable value
- Confirmed invoice correction
- Collected revenue
- Material cost
- Gross margin
- Review and implementation labor

Do not describe gross charge value as profit.

## Domain validation
Have a qualified HVAC operator or billing decision-maker review synthetic and real-world examples before using findings commercially.

Contractor-specific pricebook mappings take precedence over generic terminology, but must be versioned and attributable.
